What VoltTune does

Everything your scooter
is capable of.

VoltTune sends direct BLE commands using the same protocol as the official apps — without the artificial restrictions.

⚡
Sport Mode
Removes the firmware speed cap and maximises motor power. Works on all supported models.
🚀
Zero Start
Instant throttle response from standstill. No more push-off required.
🎚
Custom Speed Limit
Set any speed from 5 km/h up to the hardware maximum with a smooth slider and quick presets.
🌿
Eco Mode
Reduce motor power to extend battery range. Mutually exclusive with Sport mode.
🔒
Cruise Control
Locks speed so you can ride hands-light. Release throttle to cancel.
🚨
Panic Button
Configure a scooter button to instantly cap speed — great for handing your scooter to someone else.
🔐
Lock & Alarm
Lock the scooter and arm the alarm remotely via BLE on supported models.
🔧
Controller Info
Reads firmware version, motor wattage and controller type directly from BLE registers.
🎨
Themes
Choose your accent color — white, Segway orange, blue, purple — or enable Rainbow mode. Custom hex supported.

Installation

Install on iPhone.
No Mac needed.

VoltTune is a free IPA file. Install it using AltStore — a free tool that lets you sideload any app on your iPhone without a Mac or paid developer account.

01
Install AltStore on your iPhone
Download AltServer for Windows on your PC. Connect your iPhone via USB. Open AltServer (tray icon) → Install AltStore → select your iPhone. Sign in with any free Apple ID.
No paid Apple Developer account needed. A free Apple ID is enough.
02
Download VoltTune.ipa to your iPhone
Tap the download button below. In Safari, tap and hold the link → "Download Linked File". Or send the .ipa from your PC to iPhone via AirDrop or the Files app.
03
Install with AltStore
Open AltStore on iPhone → tap + (bottom right) → select VoltTune.ipa. AltStore signs and installs the app. Your iPhone must be on the same Wi-Fi as your PC, or connected via USB.
04
Trust the developer certificate
Go to iPhone Settings → General → VPN & Device Management. Tap your Apple ID → tap Trust. One-time step.
05
Open VoltTune and connect your scooter
Open VoltTune → tap Connect → your scooter appears in the list → tap it → tap Connect. If prompted, press the power button on your scooter to pair. Start tuning.
AltStore-signed apps expire every 7 days. Keep AltServer on your PC running on the same Wi-Fi to auto-refresh. Or use AltStore+ ($1.49/month) for wireless refresh.
